01 — About

I'm a hardware engineer with a soft spot for the software side. My work spans circuit design, embedded systems, and the messy, satisfying process of turning an idea into something you can hold in your hands.

I've spent years working on everything from sensor arrays and motor controllers to IoT systems that need to be reliable in the real world — not just on a bench. I care a lot about the craft of engineering and even more about the people doing it.

I started my tech podcast because I wanted to have the conversations that don't happen at conferences — the honest ones about process, failure, and what it actually takes to ship hardware.
